Resources & Tips
How to use your plan
Is this a guaranteed career prediction?

No. It is a decision-support tool. Career outcomes depend on performance, eligibility, preferences, changing admission rules and many factors not captured here.

How should I use the college shortlist?

Treat it as a starting point. Always check the institution's official admission page for the current year, eligibility, entrance route, fees, deadlines and counselling rules.

How are careers scored?

The model compares your profile with learned career-profile examples using nearest-neighbour similarity. Explicit feedback can add new local training examples. It is not a validated psychometric assessment.
